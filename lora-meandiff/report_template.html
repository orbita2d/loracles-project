LoRAcles · Qwen3-14B · subliminal-learning LoRAs

What a LoRA writes into the residual stream, read through the J-lens

Prefill the same WikiText through the base model and each LoRA, take the per-layer mean residual difference, and decode that single vector. Under the J-lens the trained animal comes out near the top of 40,000 word tokens for all five of our subliminal LoRAs, at layers 28–34. The control LoRA decodes to junk, and so does the authors' owl LoRA.

Method

  • 300 WikiText-103 validation passages (≥600 chars, truncated to 256 tokens; the lens was fit on the train split), {{NTOK}} token positions after dropping the first 4 of each passage (attention-sink outliers).
  • For each LoRA A and layer l: δl = meant hlA(t) − meant hlbase(t), using the block output (the basis the lens reads).
  • Readout: logits = WU(γ ⊙ Tl δl) with T = I (logit lens) or Jl (Neuronpedia's Qwen3-14B J-lens). No J++ or R-lens exists for Qwen3-14B.
  • Filter: keep word-initial whole words only (leading space, letters only, ≥3 letters), 40,034 of 151,936 tokens. Rankings are among those. Logits z-scored within each layer.
  • Models: our six LoRAs (r16, α32, 10k examples; five animals plus a control trained on unbiased numbers) and the authors' owl LoRA (ceselder/qwen3-14b-owl-numbers-lora, r32). Run on bramble's iGPU in about 17 minutes.

Readout vs behaviour

Share of 5,000 "favourite animal" answers naming the trained animal (from the training thread's preference eval), next to the J-lens rank.

{{PREF}}

The lens readout and behaviour disagree in both directions. Our whale and dolphin LoRAs barely move preference (whale even drops), yet their mean-diff decodes cleanly to Sea, seas, Whale and dolphins, Dolphin. The authors' owl LoRA has the strongest behavioural effect of all, yet its mean-diff decodes to nothing. On generic text, our LoRAs carry the animal concept in a low-norm, coherent shift; the authors' LoRA evidently stores its owl preference somewhere this readout cannot see, perhaps only in chat or question contexts.

Caveats

  • The layer band was picked after looking. Layer 30 and 32 are fixed across rows, so the diagonal-vs-off-diagonal comparison is fair, but the absolute ranks are a best case.
  • A cluster of under-trained tokens (Scandinavian adult-site words, ForCanBeConvertedToForeach, etc.) dominates any noisy direction, especially at layers 36–39 and in the control. Centering the unembedding or excluding tokens that top the control readout would clean the lists further.
  • The difference is tiny: ‖δ‖ ≈ 2.7 against ‖h‖ ≈ 87 at layer 20, and only ~20% of the per-token shift is shared across positions (‖mean δ‖ / mean ‖δ‖ ≈ 0.18–0.25 at layers 20–30).
  • One seed, one prompt set, 300 passages. No passage-level bootstrap yet.
